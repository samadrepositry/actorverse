Privacy Policy — ACTORVERSE

Last updated: 25 November 2026

ACTORVERSE ("the app") is an actor-training app by Actorverse (developer: Abdul Samad, contact email: contactasrind@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. What the app is

ACTORVERSE helps you train as an actor: guided exercises, a teleprompter that records you while you read, camera and mirror studios, a practice timer, monologues, training plans, a script vault for your PDFs, an AI Coach, progress tracking, a community, and an audition center.

2. Information the app collects

Account information. When you create an account, the app collects your name and email address. You need an account to use the app, so your practice data can be saved to your account.

Recordings you make. The app uses your phone's camera and microphone only when you choose to record (Teleprompter, Camera Studio, Voice Studio). The video and audio you record are saved to your account so you can watch them back in Studio → My Private Recordings. The app does not record anything without you pressing Record.

Scripts and files you upload. PDF scripts you add to the Script Vault are stored in your account. Photos you take for the Teleprompter's Picture Prompt or Scan Monologue features are used to show you the picture or to read the words on the page, and the scanned text is saved as your script text.

Practice activity. The app records your practice sessions, timers, streaks, exercise progress, plan enrolments and achievements, so it can show you your progress.

Device and technical information. The app may collect basic technical information needed to run the service, such as the app version and error reports.

The app does NOT collect your precise location, your contacts, or your photos other than the ones you deliberately take or upload inside the app.

3. How the information is used

  • To run the app and keep your account, recordings, scripts and progress saved for you.
  • To show you your own practice history and progress.
  • To operate the features you use (for example, reading the words from a page you photograph for Scan Monologue).
  • To keep the service secure and to fix errors.
  • To answer you if you contact support.

4. AI Coach — current state

The AI Coach answers through Google Gemini. When you ask the Coach a question, your question and the recent messages in that chat are sent to Google Gemini so it can generate the answer. Your recordings, scripts and account details are NOT sent to Gemini. Gemini is provided by Google; its handling of what you type is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Analytics and advertising

The app collects usage analytics from your own practice data inside the app (which features you use, session times, streaks, and progress) to show you your Analytics page and to improve the app. No third-party analytics tracker is used and no usage data is sent to advertisers. The app shows no advertising.

6. What is shared with others

The app does not sell your information and does not share your recordings, scripts or account details with third parties, except:

  • With the hosting providers that run the app's servers, only to operate the service: Vercel (hosts the app) and Supabase (hosts the database and file storage where your account data, recordings and scripts are kept).
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of users and the service.

Your recordings and scripts are private to your account. They are not shown to other users. Community posts you choose to publish are visible as you post them.

7. How your information is stored and protected

Your information is stored on the app's secure servers and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to the servers is limited to what is needed to operate the service. No method of storage is completely secure, so the app cannot promise absolute security, but it takes reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. How long information is kept

Your account data is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, your recordings, scripts and practice data are deleted with it (see section 9).

9. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can view, play and delete your own recordings and scripts inside the app (Studio → My Private Recordings; Scripts → Script Vault).
  • You can ask for your account and all of its data to be deleted by emailing contactasrind@gmail.com from the email address of the account. Account deletion is permanent.
  • You can stop the app using the camera or microphone at any time in your phone's app permissions; recording features will then not work until you allow them again.

10. Children's privacy

ACTORVERSE is for users of all ages — there is no age restriction. The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without parental consent, email contactasrind@gmail.com and the account will be removed.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address before it takes effect, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Important changes (for example, connecting an outside AI service or adding analytics) will be noted in the app as well.
